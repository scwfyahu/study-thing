HOW TO START (no terminal needed)

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (Desktop is fine).
2. Double-click StudyThing.exe.
3. Your browser opens the app. That's it.

First brain setup — pick ONE:

  OPTION A (private, recommended):
    1. Install Ollama (free): https://ollama.com/download — double-click the installer.
    2. After it installs, open the Ollama app once.
    3. In the app or any terminal run:  ollama pull qwen3:8b
       (one time, ~5 GB download)
    Done. Everything runs on your PC, nothing leaves it.

  OPTION B (cloud, faster on weak PCs):
    1. Get a free key: https://openrouter.ai/keys
    2. Open the ".env" file next to StudyThing.exe in Notepad.
    3. Replace the line with:
         OPENROUTER_API_KEY=sk-or-...yourkey...
         STUDY_LLM_PROVIDER=openrouter
         STUDY_OPENROUTER_MODEL=google/gemini-2.5-flash
    4. Save. Restart StudyThing.exe.

Notes:
- Keep the black window open while using the app — closing it quits StudyThing.
- Your recordings, notes and flashcards are stored in the "data" folder next to the exe.
- First transcription of a recording uses the bundled Whisper model (already included,
  nothing to download).
- To update: download a newer StudyThing-windows.zip, replace everything EXCEPT the
  "data" folder and your ".env" file.
